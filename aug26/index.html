Scope. Every article indexed in PubMed/MEDLINE for JAAD 92(6) & 93(1), Australas J Dermatol 66(4) & 66(5), and Br J Dermatol 192(4), 192(5) & 192(6) was retrieved, then filtered to continuing-medical-education articles, narrative and clinical reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, guidelines and consensus statements. Section headings (Continuing Medical Education, Clinical Reviews, Review Article, Evidence-Based Dermatology…) were read from each publisher's own table of contents, since PubMed does not carry them. Original research, case reports, correspondence and editorials fall outside this month's scope.

Two notes. The Australasian Journal of Dermatology published no July 2025 issue — volume 66 runs Feb, Mar, May, Jun, Aug, Sep, Nov, Dec — so June (66/4) and August (66/5) cover the requested window. Three BJD 192(4) papers marked In July list also appeared in the July 2026 reading list, where that issue was listed as "online in Feb–Mar".
