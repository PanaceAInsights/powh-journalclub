Scope. Every article indexed in PubMed/MEDLINE for JAAD 93(2) & 93(3), Australas J Dermatol 66(5) & 66(6), and Br J Dermatol 193(2) & 193(3) was retrieved, then filtered to continuing-medical-education articles, narrative and clinical reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, scoping reviews, guidelines and consensus / position statements. Section headings (Continuing Medical Education, Reviews, Review Article, Systematic Review, Guidelines…) were read from each publisher's own table of contents, since PubMed does not carry them. Original research, case reports, correspondence and editorials fall outside this month's scope.

Worth flagging. The Australasian Journal of Dermatology skips months — volume 66 runs Feb, Mar, May, Jun, Aug, Sep, Nov and Dec — so 66(5) is the August and 66(6) the September 2025 issue. Because AJD has no July issue, its August number (66/5) was also carried in the August 2026 edition, so those AJD papers recur here by design. Headline items this round include the AAD focused update to the atopic-dermatitis guidelines, the ISCL/EORTC/USCLC paediatric mycosis-fungoides consensus, the DEBRA epidermolysis-bullosa oesophageal-stricture guideline, and two Australasian College of Dermatologists statements on AI in practice.
