How each edition is built

Every article indexed in PubMed/MEDLINE for the target issues is retrieved through the NCBI E-utilities API, then filtered to that month's scope. Section headings — Continuing Medical Education, Clinical Reviews, Evidence-Based Dermatology — are read from each publisher's own table of contents, because PubMed does not carry them.

Screening & reading

The dashboard filters by journal, evidence type, topic and issue, and lets you mark each paper Include / Maybe / Exclude, flag one as presenting and add notes — with keyboard triage (j k i m x). Decisions are stored in your browser only; use Export decisions to keep a copy.

Citing

Bibliographic data and abstracts are © the respective publishers and indexed by the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Every entry links to its DOI and PubMed record — always cite the primary source.
