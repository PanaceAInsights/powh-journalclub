CME, Reviews & Guidelines for the November 2026 meeting

Scope. Every article indexed in PubMed/MEDLINE for JAAD 93(5), 93(6) & 94(1), Australas J Dermatol 66(7) & 66(8), and Br J Dermatol 193(5), 193(6) & 194(1) was retrieved (500 records), then filtered to continuing-medical-education articles, narrative and clinical reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, scoping reviews, guidelines and consensus statements. Section headings (Continuing Medical Education, Clinical Review, Original Articles, Brief Reports, Health Policy & Practice, Review Article, Guidelines…) were read from each publisher's own table of contents, since PubMed does not carry them. Original research, case reports, correspondence and editorials fall outside this month's scope.

Worth flagging. The November 2025 issues were already in the October edition, and so was AJD's December issue (66/8), which stood in for AJD's missing October number. That makes 24 of these 49 papers repeats, each tagged In Oct list. To give the meeting enough new material, January 2026 was added for JAAD and BJD (AJD has no January issue; its next is February 2026), so 25 papers are new: 12 from December and 13 from January. The Edition filter separates new from repeat, and if you screened the repeats in the October dashboard on this browser, a banner offers to bring those decisions across. New this round: two-part JAAD CMEs on lentigo maligna and on augmented intelligence in dermatology, the BAD/BSR living guideline on Behçet's, EORTC/USCLC/ISCL consensus for the cutaneous lymphoproliferative disorders, the International Psoriasis Council's definition of topical-therapy failure, Delphi consensus on outcomes after hidradenitis suppurativa surgery and on cosmetic skincare ingredients, a clinical review of low-dose naltrexone, and BJD reviews of anti-p200 pemphigoid, alloknesis and the genetic half of the mosaic pigmentation series.

Papers without an abstract. Fourteen papers have no journal abstract: eleven new (ten JAAD brief reports and health-policy pieces, plus BJD's Behçet's guideline) and three repeats from October. Every one now carries a synopsis compiled from the full text, read through institutional access, so October's preview-only notes are replaced. Five also carry an appraisal note on something worth a second look: text that contradicts its own tables (upadacitinib in dermatomyositis, punch excision for keloids, ChatGPT accuracy), shaky statistics (rosacea adverse events) or sponsorship (bimekizumab).

Left out on purpose. JAAD's Dermatology Images atlases on leprosy (December) and cutaneous melanoma (January), as with October's atlases; its two Global Burden of Disease analyses (alopecia areata in the United States, non-melanoma skin cancer worldwide), which are modelling studies rather than evidence syntheses; the Academy's response to the 2023 allergy-immunology atopic dermatitis guideline (From the Academy, a commentary rather than a guideline); BJD's perspectives (skin signs of T-cell malignancies after CAR T-cell therapy, the ILDS skin disease atlases, chatbots for delusional infestation); and its research letter on failed pemphigoid and pemphigus trials. The November issues were not re-curated: the repeats are October's selection unchanged, apart from the new synopses.
